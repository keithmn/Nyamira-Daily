Since devolution began, Nyamira County has spent about **KSh 59.6 billion**. That is the total of the Controller of Budget's actual spending figures for the twelve financial years from 2013/14 to 2024/25.

The Controller of Budget is an independent office that checks what counties spend. Its figures can differ from the county's own reports; where they do, we use the Controller of Budget's and show the other figure.

## Where the money went

- **About KSh 46.0 billion (77%) was recurrent spending**: salaries, allowances, fuel, rent and supplies, the cost of running the county every day.
- **About KSh 13.6 billion (23%) was development spending**: roads, classrooms, dispensaries, water projects and markets, the things meant to last.

These two parts add up to slightly more than the total because each year's figures are rounded in the reports.

## Spending grew, development did not

In 2013/14 the county spent KSh 2.4 billion. In 2023/24 it spent KSh 6.17 billion, its highest year so far. Development spending did not grow in the same way: it was KSh 1.4 billion in 2020/21 and KSh 1.05 billion in 2024/25.

The share of the development budget the county actually used has ranged from **27.9%** (2017/18) to **65.3%** (2020/21). In 2024/25 it was **37%**. See [what "development budget used" means](/explainers/development-budget/).

## What this does not tell us

- Spending figures show money paid, not whether the work was done well, or done at all. For that, see each ward's projects and, as our field checks grow, what is actually on the ground.
- Approved budgets are not added up here: unspent money is often carried into the next year and budgeted again, so adding budgets would count the same shillings twice.
- From 2022/23, hospital fees are counted as local revenue, so revenue before and after that change cannot be compared directly.

## Questions to ask

- Why has development spending stayed around KSh 1 to 1.4 billion a year while total spending grew?
- Which development projects in my ward were budgeted but not paid for, and will they be budgeted again?
- Why do the county's own reports for 2024/25 give a different total from the Controller of Budget?
