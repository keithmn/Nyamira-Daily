A county budget has two parts.

**Recurrent money** pays for the county's running costs: salaries, fuel, rent and supplies.

**Development money** pays for things that last: roads, classrooms, dispensaries, water projects and markets.

The Controller of Budget, an independent office, checks how much of each part a county actually spends. The share of development money spent is often called **absorption**.

In 2024/25, Nyamira used **37 per cent** of its development budget: **KSh 1.05 billion**. The rest was planned and approved, but not paid for that year.

## Why it can be low

A low share does not on its own prove that money was lost. Money from the national government can arrive late in the year, tenders can take months, or a project may not be ready to pay for. Ask the county which of these applies.

## What you can ask

- Which projects in my ward were budgeted but not started?
- When will they be paid for?
- Will the money be budgeted again next year?

Ask at your ward's budget forum, or write to the county. The [Ask Nyamira](/ask/) page shows how.
