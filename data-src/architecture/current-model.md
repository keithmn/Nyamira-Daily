## Structural problems

1. **Duplicated facts.** Kisii FY2024/25 budget is in `compare.json` (15,155.35m) and `counties/kisii/money.json` (15,160m, rounded). Two values for one year; the page reads one, the test does not compare them.
2. **Links by name, not ID.** Census and Kisii leaders use ward slugs, but `ngcdf.json` uses constituency names, `leaders.json` national offices use "MP, <name>" text, and `wardspend.json` and `audit.json` use `ward` in different formats. Contract `wards` may hold slugs or names; this is not checked.
3. **No stable ID for places, people or towns.** `towns.json` has no id; `ward-places.json` ids are not referenced by facilities or living events. Living events cannot yet link to anything stable.
4. **Two facility ID schemes.** NYM-F…, KSI-H…, KSI-P… are not joined to ward-places or towns. Duplicate names across lists are possible.
5. **Mixed-county records.** `contracts.json` and `wardspend.json` have no county field; contracts mix county, national, NG-CDF and roads in one file (`group`). `roads.json` has no county field.
6. **Schemas differ by county.** Nyamira `money.json` has no `sha256`, `page` is free text; Kisii has `sha256` and `note`. Nyamira `leaders.json` and Kisii `leaders.json` have the same shape but the Kisii file has partly null names.
7. **Hard-coded county in pages.** 48 page and component files contain the word "Nyamira"; titles and labels are not driven by data.
8. **Derived files committed.** `geo.json`, `roads.json`, `ward-register.json` are generated; `geo.json` was extended by an ad hoc script (`scripts/geo/add-county-geo.mjs`) not covered by a test.
9. **Empty tables.** `facility-observations.json`, `road-conditions.json`, `living/events.json` hold no records, so dated-event logic is untested on real data.

## Not covered by the sanity test

The test (`scripts/data-integrity.test.js`) checks facility sources, labels and wards, facility id uniqueness, money source fields, money spend against budget, Kisii MCA and census keys, and map shapes. It does not check: contracts, roads, wardspend, audit, ngcdf, census (Nyamira), compare.json against money files (duplicate facts), towns and ward-places, living events, content collection links (`ward`, `sector`), or county fields on mixed records.
