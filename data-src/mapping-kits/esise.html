At each facility:
  1. Open KoboCollect → Place visit form
  2. Select Esise ward and the facility name
  3. Stand at the entrance. Wait for GPS accuracy under 10 metres
  4. Record: open/closed, condition, services, water, power
  5. Take one photo of the outside (no people in the photo)
  6. Tick the privacy box and submit
  7. Tick ☐ on this sheet when done

If the facility is not on the list: choose "Not on the list" in KoboCollect, type the name as written on its sign, and select its type.
