# Nyamira roads: which public body is responsible: notes

Researched 29 Oct 2026 by an assistant (web research; Kenya Law was blocked to automated access, so the Act and rulings were read from a mirror and news). Not yet checked by an editor: re-check each source before publishing anything from it. Confidence: **High** means I read it in the primary document. **Medium** means it comes from a primary document I could only partly read, or from an official mirror. **Low** means it is news-reported or secondary.

## Bottom line

- **There is no public, downloadable road-by-road register for Nyamira that names the responsible body.**
  - The official register was gazetted in January 2016 (Kenya Gazette Supplement No. 4 of 2016). I could not get a copy.
  - KRB's "Roads Register" page is password-protected.
  - KRB's web map shows the register but offers no download.
- **The best citable road-level source is KRB's Annual Public Roads Programme (APRP) FY 2024/25.** It names roads in Nyamira by code under KeNHA, KeRRA and KURA, with surface type. KeNHA and KURA rows also give condition. It only covers roads in that year's work plan, not the whole network.
- **The legal split is unsettled.**
  - On 5 Jun 2025 the High Court reportedly found the classification section of the Kenya Roads Act (s.47) unconstitutional. The Court of Appeal stayed that ruling for 12 months on 25 Jul 2025.
  - The bill meant to reclassify roads into "national trunk" and "county" was rejected by the Senate on 4 Aug 2026 and sent to mediation.
  - The January 2016 classification is still the latest gazetted one I could find.
- **Who works on a road is not always who is legally responsible for it.**
  - KeRRA spends road-levy money on county-class roads: county roads make up 60% of KeRRA's planned km in 2024/25.
  - KeRRA says that for roads outside its network it gets a no-objection or MoU from the county.
  - The site should show the class-based responsible body and the agency that funded the works as separate facts.

## Q1. Legal classification and responsibility since devolution

| Road class | Responsible body (as applied 2024/25) | Source | Sure? |
|---|---|---|---|
| National trunk roads (all) | National government | Constitution 2010, Fourth Schedule Part 1 item 18(b): "the construction and operation of national trunk roads". Item 18(c) gives national government "standards for the construction and maintenance of other roads by counties" | High |
| County roads (all) | County governments | Constitution 2010, Fourth Schedule Part 2 item 5(a): "county roads" | High |
| S, A, B | KeNHA | KRB APRP 2024/25, s.1.3, PDF p.8 (printed p.7) | High |
| C (national trunk, "primary roads") | KeRRA | KRB APRP 2024/25, s.1.3, PDF p.8; KeRRA Annual Report FY2024/25, PDF p.31 (28,150 km "out of 56,289 km of national trunk roads") | High |
| Urban roads in cities and municipalities (UA/UB/UC) | KURA | Kenya Roads Act 2007 s.10(1): "all public roads in the cities and municipalities in Kenya except where those roads are national roads"; APRP Table 1 | High (APRP), Medium (Act text) |
| Roads in national parks and national game reserves | KWS | APRP s.1.3. APRP note iv (PDF p.21): KWS maintains them "on behalf of the Road Authorities and County Governments" (1% of the road levy) | High |
| D, E, F, G (G = unclassified) | County governments | APRP s.1.3 ("County Roads (Classes D and below)"); KRB booklet *Custodians of Kenya's Roads* (2022) PDF p.5 ("all roads (rural and Urban) in classes D, E, F and G"); KNBS Economic Survey 2026 Table 11.10, PDF p.315 | High |

Class definitions (S to F) are printed under KNBS Economic Survey 2026 Table 11.10 (PDF p.315). That table groups S/A/B/C as "National Roads" and D/E/F/G as "County Roads", with G as "Unclassified Roads". Its source is KRB.

**Kenya Roads Act 2007** (Cap. 408). This text comes from the sheriaplex.com mirror of the 2007 Act. Kenya Law's official site blocks automated access, so I could not read the consolidated version. Confidence Medium.

- **The Act still uses pre-devolution terms.** s.4(1): KeNHA covers "national roads". s.7(1): KeRRA covers "rural roads". s.10(1): KURA covers urban roads.
- **s.2** defines each of those terms "as classified in the First Schedule". I could not retrieve the First Schedule text.
- **s.47** says roads are classified per the First Schedule. Under s.47(5) an Authority may add, modify or remove a road's category with the Minister's authority published in the Gazette.
- **s.48** requires each Authority to keep an inventory of its roads, to be published in the Gazette.
- **Amendments.** Act No. 3 of 2022 took effect 1 Mar 2023; I did not verify what it changed. Act No. 13 of 2025 (26 Aug 2025) reportedly only changed the Directors-General's terms (s.13).

**2016 gazetted classification.** KRB's map portal says the network classification was "gazetted in January 2016 (Kenya Gazette Supplement No. 4 of 2016)", with two groups: National Trunk Roads and County Roads. KeRRA's annual report cites it as the "Kenya Roads Register, 2016" (161,457 km). KeNHA's profile cites "L.N. 2/2016" (secondary source).

- I could not retrieve the notice itself.
- KRB ran stakeholder validation of an updated register in July 2022 and February 2023 (KRB public notice, 6 Feb 2023).
- KRB's map now shows a "Revised Road Classification (2021)" layer and a "Proposed Road Register 2025". I found no gazettement of either.

**Litigation.**

- **High Court, 5 Jun 2025, Justice Mugambi** (Kenya Law ref. [2025] KEHC 7992): found s.47 of the Kenya Roads Act and s.6 of the KRB Act unconstitutional (The Standard, 5 Jun 2025). The Star reports the CS and AG were told to reclassify all roads within 12 months.
- **Court of Appeal, 25 Jul 2025** ([2025] KECA 1366): stayed the judgment for 12 months.
- I could not open either ruling. Confidence Low to Medium. I found no confirmed outcome after July 2026.

**Pending bill.** Kenya Roads (Amendment) (No. 3) Bill, 2025 (NA Bill No. 34 of 2025).

- **Proposed classes.** It would split roads into national trunk roads and county roads.
  - National roads: As, A and B. Urban roads: Au, Bu and Cu. Rural roads: C, SP, SR and LM.
  - County roads: D, E, Du and Eu.
  - Signs would have to show each road's class and the level of government responsible (Senate Bill Digest).
- **Progress.** The National Assembly passed it on 7 Oct 2025. The Senate rejected the Second Reading on 4 Aug 2026, which commits the bill to a Mediation Committee under Article 112(1)(a) (National Assembly Votes and Proceedings, 25 Aug 2026). It is **not law**. High.

## Q2. Published lists or datasets classifying Nyamira's roads

- **Gazetted register (Jan 2016).** Exists but was not obtained. The KRB register page (https://krb.go.ke/roads-register/) is password-protected. Not usable yet.
- **KRB map portal** (https://maps.krb.go.ke). Interactive maps with these layers: classification (2016, plus "Revised 2021" and proposed classes), conditions (RICS 2023 and 2018), surface type, and "Proposed Road Register 2025". The data are view-only, with no download, and its terms say the data stay KRB property. I could not read Nyamira attributes from it (JavaScript map). Usable only to cross-check by eye.
- **KRB APRP FY 2024/25** (Jul 2025, downloadable PDF). Road-level rows for Nyamira:
  - KeNHA: code, section, surface, condition, planned km (PDF p.52).
  - KeRRA: Nyamira region by constituency, with code, name, surface and km, but no condition (PDF pp.88–89, 163–165).
  - KURA: Nyamira town, with KURA section code, surface, condition and km (PDF pp.203–204).
  - Usable: yes, as "roads in the 2024/25 work plan", not a full register. I found no 2025/26 edition online.
- **KNBS Economic Survey 2026** (Apr 2026, PDF). Table 11.9 (PDF p.313), Class C rural roads (KeRRA) in Nyamira; source KeRRA:

  | Year | Paved km | Unpaved km | Total km |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 2023 | 49.3 | 96.2 | 145.5 |
  | 2024 | 54.8 | 90.7 | 145.5 |
  | 2025 (provisional) | 63.6 | 196.3 | 259.9 |

  KNBS says the 2025 jump nationally came from the 2025 RICS mapping previously unmapped roads. Usable: yes (official, county-level).
- **Nyamira CIDP 2023–2027** (County Government, Aug 2023, PDF). Usable in part: the county-level class totals, with the caveats below.
  - **Named roads with codes** (printed p.176, PDF p.191).
  - **Map 1 "Road Classification"** (printed p.178, PDF p.193) has a length table: B 114.37, C 158.30, D 110.24, E 121.03, F 61.18, G 574.52, total 1,139.64 km.
    - I read these from a low-resolution image; the six values add up exactly to the printed total.
    - The map gives no data date or source.
  - **Text figures** (printed p.179): "about 20.1% of the classified roads" are bitumen; "about 27.6% of the roads are unclassified and are mainly earth roads"; in Bosamaro ward "all the roads are earth roads".
  - **Missing tables.** The contents list Table 12 ("Roads classification per Constituency") and Table 13 ("Paved roads"), but they are missing from the PDF (broken cross-references).
  - **The county itself lists "Unclear classification of roads" as a problem** and plans a road inventory survey (printed p.128).
- **Nyamira County Annual Development Plan 2026/27** (Sept 2025). No network statistics. It repeats "Carry road inventory survey of all roads in the county and Policy review on road classification" (PDF p.108), and lists ward roads opened, graded or gravelled by the county. Not usable for classification.
- **KeNHA, KeRRA and KURA registers.** I found no public per-county register from any of them. Their websites and annual reports give national totals only: KeRRA has 28,150 km; the APRP gives KeNHA 22,320 km and KURA 3,826 km. KeRRA's Nyamira tender notices exist, but the PDFs I tried were unreachable (404 or blocked).
- **The RICS 2023 report itself** was not found online. Its results appear only in summary (APRP Fig. 1; the KRB map).

**The county's own list** (CIDP printed p.176):

- **Main roads:** "Kisii-Chemosit road (B4) and Konate-Senta Road (B5)".

**KWS.** The APRP 2024/25 KWS work plan has no roads in Nyamira; its Western region covers Ruma, Mt Elgon, Saiwa and others. High for that work plan.

## Q4. Official condition data and scale definitions

**Road-level condition.** Only in the APRP 2024/25:

- **KeNHA rows:** Good or Fair (table above). The APRP says KeNHA's plan uses its 2023 Annual Road Inventory and Condition Survey (ARICS), PDF p.29.
- **KURA Nyamira rows:**
  - Good: paved sections of 0.70–0.76 km.
  - Fair: gravel sections.
  - Poor: one 0.40 km section of Nyamira–Siamani Rd_1, planned for upgrading to bitumen.
- **KeRRA rows have no condition field.**

**County-level condition.** None found in numbers. The CIDP is qualitative: most gravel roads are "in poor condition", and earth roads are impassable in the rains (printed p.179). KRB's conditions map (RICS 2023/2018) would hold it, but it cannot be downloaded.

**National mix only (2023 RICS):**

| Good | Fair | Poor | Under construction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 22.77% | 46.35% | 29.62% | 1.26% |

Source: APRP Figure 1, PDF p.7. The total network was 239,105 km. KRB calls Good + Fair the "maintainable network", 68% in 2023.

**Scale definitions: not found.** No public KRB, RICS or ARICS document I could reach defines Good, Fair or Poor (no IRI or visual thresholds).

- The APRP only links categories to treatments. Good and fair roads get routine, periodic or performance-based maintenance. Poor roads get spot improvement, rehabilitation or reconstruction (PDF p.20).
- KRB's 2022 booklet uses "good condition and is maintainable" versus "requires rehabilitation or reconstruction".
- The "Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor" scale in KeRRA's annual report is a performance-contract score, **not** road condition. Do not use it.

## Cautions for the site

1. **Record "responsible body" with its basis.** For example: "KeNHA (Class B, per KRB APRP 2024/25)" or "County Government of Nyamira (Class D–G roads, per KRB)". Where no source names the road, show "unknown". Do not infer a class from a road's surface or from which agency funded the works.
2. **Treat the 2016 classification as the current gazetted basis**, and note that it is under court challenge and a reclassification bill is in mediation.
3. **Flag conflicting figures as questions to ask.** Class C in Nyamira is 158.30 km on the CIDP map (undated) but 145.5 km (2023–24) or 259.9 km (2025, provisional) in KNBS/KeRRA.
4. **Do not use road codes as unique IDs.** Codes in KeRRA work plans repeat across different roads.

The documents are at the URLs in the source table; none are stored in this repository.
