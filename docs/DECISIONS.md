# Decisions log

Why the site is built the way it is. Add new decisions at the bottom with a date. If you want to reverse one, discuss it first.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Static site with Astro 5**, plain HTML output | Fast on cheap phones and slow networks, free to host (Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages), nothing to hack or keep running. Astro 5 because the build environment's Node version did not support Astro 7. |
| 2 | **System fonts, no web fonts, minimal JavaScript** (fonts superseded by #19) | Pages are around 10–30 KB. Data costs matter to readers. |
| 3 | **Two-layer pages**: one number, one sentence, one label first; detail behind "See the detail" | Most readers stop after the first screen. They should still leave with something true. |
| 4 | **Evidence labels** on every figure (Verified, Audit finding, Official claim, Unverified) | Trust. See `EDITORIAL_RULES.md`. |
| 5 | **Informational, not news-heavy** | The site is a reference people return to, not a feed. Short factual updates only. |
| 6 | **Maps as inline SVG generated at build time**, no map library, no Google Maps API | Free, no API keys or usage limits, works without JavaScript, very light. Google Places was used only to discover names, never stored as coordinates (licence terms). Interactive maps (MapLibre/Leaflet + OpenStreetMap) can come later if needed. |
| 7 | **Ward pages and a site-wide ward filter, both** | The filter ("Your ward" picker, saved in localStorage) makes the whole site about the reader's ward. Ward pages remain because they can be shared on WhatsApp, found on Google, and work without JavaScript. |
| 8 | **Ward receipt** at the top of each ward page, with a WhatsApp share button | Most readers arrive from WhatsApp links; a short, forwardable summary is the main entry point. |
| 9 | **No "Coming soon" boxes.** Unfinished pages are tagged "Planned" in menus and left off the home page | Empty boxes make a site look abandoned. The list of planned pages is in `src/config.js`. |
| 10 | **Tables become stacked cards on phones** (`table.cards`) | No sideways scrolling on small screens. |
| 11 | **Share and Print at the bottom of pages**, not the top | Content first. |
| 12 | **"Tell us" and "Get updates" hidden until a contact channel exists** | Set `contact.whatsapp` or `contact.email` in `src/config.js` to turn them on. |
| 13 | **Language policy:** English written backbone; short Kiswahili summary on key pages; full Kiswahili and Ekegusii later; audio in the speaker's language with subtitles | Reach without tripling the editing load at the start. Kiswahili summaries need review by a native speaker before launch. |
| 14 | **Questions, not accusations** for projects with repeat budgeting or stalled works | See `EDITORIAL_RULES.md` section 3. |
| 15 | **Charts drawn at build time** (`src/components/BarChart.astro`) with one axis, native tooltips and the same numbers in a table | Accessible, light, no chart library. Series colours are tokens in `global.css` (light and dark). |
| 16 | **No regex literals in `.astro` frontmatter of pages that use `getStaticPaths`** | Astro's compiler mis-parsed one and failed with `Unexpected "export"`. Use helpers in `src/lib/fmt.js` instead. |
| 17 | **Dark mode supported**: follows the phone's setting, with a light/dark toggle in the header (24 Oct 2026). A small script in `Base.astro`'s `<head>` sets `data-theme` before the page draws; dark tokens are under `:root[data-theme="dark"]` in `global.css`. The reader's choice is saved in their browser; choosing the same as the phone clears it. Without JavaScript, pages are light. | Many phones default to dark mode, and some readers want to choose. The toggle takes header space, so phones narrower than about 430px show the logo symbol rather than the full logo. |
| 18 | **Brand kit v1 applied** (24 Oct 2026). Logos from `public/brand/` (header: horizontal logo, switching to the symbol under 160px; footer: reverse logo on Ink). Brand colours are `--nd-*` tokens; Signal Green for fills, marks and underlines only; Record Red only for NY Watch. Default share image `public/brand/og-default.png`. | Brand guidelines. Signal Green text on white is 2.0:1, too light to read, so text is always Ink (or White on dark). |
| 19 | **League Spartan is the one web font**: Medium (500) for text, Bold (700) for headings. Subset to Latin, self-hosted WOFF2, about 22 KB for both weights, `font-display: swap`, Medium preloaded. Varela Round stays inside the logo files only. Sizes (24 Oct 2026, reduced at the owner's request): body 16px, h1 up to 32px, h2 up to 22px, h3 17px, big figures 27px; small labels never below 13px. | The brand typeface. Small enough for cheap phones and cached after the first page. Its figures are proportional (no tabular figures), so numbers in table columns don't line up exactly. |
| 20 | **Section menus in the navigation bar** (24 Oct 2026). Each of the six sections opens a menu, under the bar and over the page, listing everything in it: the section's own page first, then its pages, with nested pages indented (The Assembly's five pages; all 20 wards under their sub-counties). The current page is a green pill. Tap or click to open (no hover menus); Escape or tapping elsewhere closes. Without JavaScript the sections are plain links. Defined once in `src/nav.js`. A secondary bar was tried first and replaced. | The site has over 30 content pages; with one plain bar most were two taps away and Opportunities was linked only from the home page. Menus reach every page in two taps without making the header taller (a second bar made it 237px on phones; now 188px). Hover menus don't work on touch screens. |
| 21 | **Colour pass: Signal Green leads** (24 Oct 2026). The ward picker sits on a Signal Green band on every page; the home page opens on an Ink panel with a green base line; headline figures, the ward receipt and the footer carry a 6px green edge; page titles get a short green bar; the ward panel and Kiswahili summaries use a light green tint; NY Watch sits on an Ink band with its reverse logo. Evidence-label colours are unchanged. | After the brand kit the site still read as grey cards on white: green appeared only as small squares and underlines. Green stays a fill or edge with Ink text on it (11:1), so contrast rules still hold. |
| 22 | **Atlas redesign** (24 Oct 2026): "an atlas for every ward". (a) The home page opens on a live county map (`src/components/HeroMap.astro`): every ward is a link to its page; a pulsing dot marks each ward with a question to ask (the curated `watch` list plus projects the county's own reports list at 0%, built at build time as `questions` in `src/lib/projects.js`, each with its sources); with JavaScript the panel beside the map steps through the questions (pause, previous, next) and pointing at a ward, or tapping it once, shows that ward's figures. Motion stops under reduced-motion. (b) "My Ward" is now **Wards**, second in the bar. (c) The section bar is pills (current section a Signal Green pill), Search and Menu are round icon buttons, and the ward picker is an Ink pill in the header (its own row on phones; in the opening panel on the home page). (d) Lists, not walls of cards: home topics are rows with a green arrow. (e) Big figures get a meter for scale with the evidence label right under them. (f) Ward receipt is a paper slip with a perforated edge; long names wrap instead of overflowing. Ward projects are rows with the reported status and a small progress bar where a percentage is given. (g) Footer: who we are, one action ("Spotted a mistake?"), one row of links, labels and map credits. (h) League Spartan stays the only font (#19). | The site is a reference people return to, organised by place. The map makes "start with your ward" the first thing people see, and the questions show the site's purpose without accusing anyone. |
| 23 | **Record Red as a secondary colour, with two meanings only** (24 Oct 2026): "the show" (NY Watch logos and badges, and an "On air now" strip across every page from 10 to 11 pm Nairobi time, set in `src/config.js` `show`) and "time is running out" (Opportunities deadlines closing within 7 days; the tag counts down from the reader's clock). Later uses: "Corrected" tags and form errors. Never on questions to ask, project status, charts, evidence labels or buttons. | Red reads as alarm. Kept to the show and to deadlines it helps readers; on projects or money it would read as an accusation, against "questions, not accusations". |
| 24 | **Colour roles and infographics** (24 Oct 2026; widens #23). Signal Green is the main colour, Record Red the secondary, Ink and White the neutrals. Red marks the critical numbers, meaning the gap (money budgeted but not spent, unpaid bills, projects at 0%, revenue short of target), plus questions to ask (the map pings), deadlines and the show. It is never used on evidence labels or with words of blame; the facts stay framed as questions. New first-layer graphics in `src/components/viz/`: "out of every KSh 100" dots, split bars, horizontal ward bars (the reader's ward outlined) and service pictograms; the Money page adds money-not-spent and unpaid-bills charts. | Many readers find percentages and tables hard. Counting dots and comparing bar lengths needs no arithmetic, and one consistent red for "the gap" shows where to look. |
| 25 | **Phase 1 of the expansion** (24 Oct 2026, `docs/EXPANSION_SPEC.md`). (a) **Seven sections:** Today · Wards · Data · Take part · Stories · Government · Learn; the Constitution pages move under Learn (addresses unchanged) so the bar fits one row on phones. (b) **Red on maps means emergency only:** question pings are white (the current one green), health points teal, market points brown; alert levels use shapes as well as colours (red triangle, amber square, blue circle; dashed outline = approximate place). Off the map red still marks gaps in charts, the NY Watch dot and the On air strip; deadlines are Ink. (c) **Content collections** (`src/content/`) for text anyone can add; `draft: true` entries (the EXAMPLE ones) are left out unless built with `ND_DRAFTS=1`. (d) **Machine-readable outputs and embeds** built with the site. (e) **QR codes** for ward sheets are generated at build time with the `qrcode` package (no code reaches readers). | Keeps red meaningful where it matters most (a red mark on a map must mean danger), keeps the site static and light, and lets non-developers publish. |
| 26 | **Phase 2: community reporting front ends** (24 Oct 2026). Reports are written in the browser and sent by WhatsApp (`contact.whatsapp`) or a form link (`reportFormUrl`); nothing is stored by the site (`TODO(service)`). Only checked reports are published, as the latest status per place, with a 90-day "may be out of date" flag; clinic checks are shown only as totals per facility. Projects get stable IDs (`scripts/project-ids.py`, deterministic from ward and normalised name, manual overrides) and their own pages, where Adopt a project photo logs appear. The three tools stay Planned until their channels are set. | Static and private by default: no personal data passes through the site, nothing unchecked is published, and the pages are ready the day a WhatsApp number or form exists. |
