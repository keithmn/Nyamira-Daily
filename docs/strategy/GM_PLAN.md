# Gusii Daily: general manager's plan

Status: **draft from the owner interview of 16 Nov 2026.** Strategy only; site changes in section 8 need the owner's approval before they are built.

## 1. Where we are going

- **In three years:** the county's fact source. When anyone in Nyamira wants a verified fact (a budget line, a clinic's status, a price), they check Gusii Daily first.
- **Year-one audiences:** youth (18 to 35) and residents in the wards. Businesses, officials and researchers are served by the same data, later.
- **Red lines:** no political money; no selling personal data; no unverified claims. Paid or sponsored content is decided case by case (rules still to write, see section 9).
- **Means:** the owner plus one to three helpers; under KSh 500,000 for 12 months; self-funded; the company stays commercial for now.
- **Success at 12 months:** places checked on site; audience; the project covering its own costs.

## 2. The facts behind the plan

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Nyamira: 605,576 people, 150,669 households (2019) | KNBS 2019 census, in `src/data/census.json` |
| About 40% of Kenyans use the internet; 69% of the country is rural | DataReportal, Digital 2026: Kenya |
| Ad reach in Kenya: TikTok 18.4M adults, Facebook 17.0M, YouTube 12.1M, Instagram 3.95M, X 2.09M | DataReportal, Digital 2026: Kenya |
| Radio reaches about 80 to 87% of media users in rural regions | Communications Authority audience report, Oct to Dec 2024, as cited by radio.co.ke |
| A proven model: Outlier Media (Detroit) answers residents' own questions by text from a public-records database | Nieman Lab, March 2018 |
| Publishing identifiable personal stories needs consent; ethnic origin and family details are sensitive data | ODPC guidance on historical and statistical purposes, 2025 |

What this means: most of the county will meet us on TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp and radio, not on the website. The website is the record that every post points back to.

## 3. Audit: keep, add, remove

**Keep (the foundation):** sourced money data (12 years and the 2025/26 budget), the contracts tracker, the place model with dated events, the Kobo field forms, evidence labels, video pages tied to places.

**The gap:** none of the 247 facilities has been checked on site. The map is the headline product and holds no visits yet. Fieldwork is the first investment.

**Add, in order:**
1. Facility visits with health and school details (staffing, drugs in stock, fees, results).
2. A weekly "Opportunities this week" round-up (hand-picked, verified, with deadlines).
3. Market prices by market, monthly (the Kobo prices form exists).
4. A business directory by ward.
5. A public coverage score for every dataset.
6. A WhatsApp number residents can ask ("what is in my ward?").

**Remove or stop:** anything not tied to a source; pages that repeat each other; work on new sections before the map has real visits.

## 4. The content engine

- **Rhythm:** one story a week at first, growing towards daily as volunteers join.
- **One story, cut for every channel:** a site page with the figures; a short vertical video (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook); an X thread; a WhatsApp Channel post; a LinkedIn post.
- **Voice:** the owner as host, English first, with Kiswahili summaries.
- **Formats:** "Money made simple" (60 seconds on one figure); a place visit (what we found, on camera); "Opportunities this week"; number of the week; a monthly ward round-up.
- **One field trip, three outputs:** the visit updates the place record, becomes the week's video, and feeds the ward round-up.

## 5. The data engine

- **Fieldwork, mixed:** the team visits and films ward by ward; a volunteer correspondent in each ward covers the rest with the Kobo form; residents report places for us to confirm.
- **Monthly document watch:** one fixed day to check the county, Controller of Budget, procurement portal and Kenya Gazette sites for new documents.
- **Coverage score, public:** each dataset shows how complete and how fresh it is ("12 of 247 places checked; last update 3 Nov").
- **Corrections rewarded:** residents who send a correction that we accept are thanked and credited in the corrections log.

## 6. Money

- **Year one:** self-funded inside the KSh 500,000 budget. YouTube and platform income is the first revenue target; it needs 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours (or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days) before it pays, and pays little at first.
- **Ready but not pushed:** the Partner with us page (data licences, commissioned research, sponsorship), under two public safeguards: no political clients; client data kept apart.
- **Honest warning:** platform income alone is unlikely to cover costs in year one. Review at month six; one labelled local sponsor or a small data job would close the gap.

## 7. Risks

| Risk | Response |
|---|---|
| Political pressure | Publish only sourced facts with evidence labels; right of reply before publishing; no political money; keep the same rules for every office and party; back up the data outside the county. |
| Legal claims | Questions, not accusations; companies not private people; a written consent record for every recording; a lawyer's review of the terms, privacy page and consent forms before the Community archive opens. |
| Team safety | Visits in pairs where possible; a letter of introduction and ID; film facilities from public areas; never confront; leave when asked and note it; a check-in message before and after each visit. |

## 8. Recommended site structure (for the owner to approve)

Modern civic sites are built around the reader's own place and question, not around the organisation's sections. Five changes, smallest first:

1. **"My ward" as the front door.** Once a reader picks a ward, the home page becomes that ward's front page: its places, its money, its contracts, its latest story. The picker already exists; this makes it the main path.
2. **Ask first.** A large search box on the home page ("What do you want to know about Nyamira?") that answers from the data: a ward, a school, a contract, a budget line.
3. **A link-in-bio page** (`/go/`) for TikTok and Instagram: the latest story, this week's opportunities, your ward, WhatsApp. Light enough for the slowest phone.
4. **Stories from data.** Rename Watch to "Stories" once text stories exist: each story is a video or article plus "the numbers behind it", listed on every place, ward and money page it touches.
5. **A coverage page** (`/about/coverage/`): how complete each dataset is, and what we have not checked yet.

Not recommended yet: an app, user accounts, comments, or an AI chat assistant. Each costs more than it returns at this size.

## 9. Still open
